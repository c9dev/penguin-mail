# mailrs privacy policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

mailrs is a desktop email client for Gmail. It runs on your own computer.
This policy explains what it does with your data.

## What mailrs accesses

With your permission, granted through Google's sign-in page, mailrs uses the
Gmail API scope `gmail.modify` to:

- read your messages, labels, and drafts, so it can show them;
- change labels, so you can archive, star, and mark mail read or unread;
- move messages to the trash;
- save drafts and send messages that you write.

mailrs does not permanently delete mail and does not change your Gmail
settings.

## Where your data goes

- Messages are downloaded from Google's servers straight to your computer
  and stored in a local database in your user folder.
- The sign-in token is stored in your desktop's keyring.
- mailrs has no server of its own. No data is sent to the developer or to
  any third party, and mailrs contains no analytics or advertising.
- The only network service mailrs talks to is Google's Gmail API. When you
  choose to load remote images in a message, your computer fetches those
  images from wherever the sender hosted them.

## Keeping and removing data

Removing an account in mailrs deletes its downloaded mail and its sign-in
token from your computer. To revoke mailrs's access on Google's side, visit
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

mailrs's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use
requirements.
